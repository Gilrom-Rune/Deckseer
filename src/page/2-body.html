    Welcome to Deckseer

    1. Turn on webcam and allow camera access. Use Chrome or Edge on a PC.
    2. Hold a card up against a plain background. A green outline means it found the card. It beeps when the card is added.
    3. Going through one set? Type its code in Set lock (like MH3). Scans get faster and pick the right printing.
    4. Cards in sleeves? Tick Sleeved cards.
    5. Can't scan a card? Type its name, or set and number like HOC 96, in the box under the camera.
    6. Building a deck? Open the Deck builder tab at the top and click New deck. For Commander, scan (or type) your commander first, then the rest. The deck builder shows every card as a big picture. When you're done, copy the list into Moxfield or anywhere else.
    7. Wrong set, printing or foil? Click the card in the Deck builder, or the ✎ next to it in your scan list or collection, and pick the right one.
    8. Looking for combos? Click Find combos in a deck, or in the Collection tab for all your cards. The first time, Deckseer downloads Commander Spellbook's combo database (about 30 MB) and keeps it on your PC.
    9. Your collection saves in this browser only. Click Backup now and then, and use Export to send it to Moxfield, Archidekt and others.

    Bring in a deck from Moxfield (or Archidekt, TappedOut, MTGGoldfish, Arena…)
    1. Open your deck on Moxfield and choose Export (in the deck's More / ⋯ menu).
    2. Copy the deck list text. Any format with one card per line works, like 1 Sol Ring (CMR) 263.
    3. Paste it below and press Import. Set codes and numbers keep your exact printings, and *F* marks foils.

      Learning from your scans

      How cards are recognised: the scanner tries what it learned from your scans first, then the AI, then the Picture library.

      Each card you confirm is remembered as it looks through your webcam, in your lighting (up to 3 looks per printing, kept only in this browser). Next time it's recognised straight away, even when the name can't be read, and the same printing is picked. Fixing a wrong scan teaches it the right card.

      Picture library

      Downloads a small picture of every card artwork from Scryfall once and keeps a compact fingerprint of each on this PC (about 40 MB). Expect roughly 15–60 minutes depending on your connection. You can pause, close the page and resume later. Each person builds their own copy.

      AI picture recognition

      An image model that has seen a picture of every card. It works on this PC only, with no internet needed once it's loaded. The files (about 50 MB) are made by the Stage 2 notebook and kept in this browser.

      Ask Claude about hard cards

      For cards nothing else can read, press C to send a photo of the card to Claude, which looks at everything on it: art, set symbol, frame, language. This uses an Anthropic API key, billed per use by Anthropic (roughly a cent per card). Get one at console.anthropic.com.

      The key is saved only in this browser on this PC and is sent only to Anthropic.

      Get the scanner ready

      Two optional downloads help Deckseer recognise cards from their picture when the name can't be read: foreign cards, foils, glare and odd fonts. You can skip them and do them later.

      1. Card pictures (Picture library, about 40 MB) ✓ Done

      Deckseer downloads a small picture of every card from Scryfall once and keeps them in this browser. It takes 15–60 minutes and runs in the background: you can use Deckseer, pause, or close it and carry on later.

      2. AI picture recognition (about 50 MB) ✓ On

      More accurate than the card pictures. It needs three files you make once, for free, with a Google Colab notebook (about an hour, mostly waiting):

      1. Open the notebook below (sign in with a Google account).
      2. Choose Runtime → Change runtime type → T4 GPU, then Runtime → Run all.
      3. When it finishes, download deckseer-embed.onnx, deckseer-index.bin and deckseer-index.json.
      4. Click Load the files here and pick all three (hold Ctrl).
